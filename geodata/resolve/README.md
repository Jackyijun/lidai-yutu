# 戈壁三区（RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017）

[![中文](https://img.shields.io/badge/lang-%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87-red.svg)](#戈壁三区resolve-ecoregions-2017) [![English](https://img.shields.io/badge/lang-English-blue.svg)](#three-gobi-ecoregions-resolve-ecoregions-2017)

`gobi_ecoregions.geojson` 是 RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 里的三个生态区：东戈壁荒漠草原（Eastern Gobi desert steppe，ECO_ID 824）、戈壁湖谷荒漠草原（Gobi Lakes Valley desert steppe，825）、阿拉善高原半荒漠（Alashan Plateau semi-desert，808）。几何取自 [CatalogueOfLife/col-gazetteers](https://github.com/CatalogueOfLife/col-gazetteers) 里 `teow/features/` 的简化版，只留名称、生物群系与许可几个字段。`tools/damo.py` 由它改绘七卷共用的「大漠」（`geodata/derived/damo.json`）。

出处：Dinerstein, E., et al. 2017. An Ecoregion-Based Approach to Protecting Half the Terrestrial Realm. *BioScience* 67(6): 534–545. https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014 。数据 © RESOLVE，CC-BY 4.0。

## Three Gobi ecoregions (RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017)

`gobi_ecoregions.geojson` holds three ecoregions of RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017: Eastern Gobi desert steppe (ECO_ID 824), Gobi Lakes Valley desert steppe (825) and Alashan Plateau semi-desert (808). The geometry is the simplified version in `teow/features/` of the [CatalogueOfLife/col-gazetteers](https://github.com/CatalogueOfLife/col-gazetteers) repository; only the name, biome and licence fields are kept. `tools/damo.py` uses it to redraw the Great Desert shared by seven volumes (`geodata/derived/damo.json`).

Source: Dinerstein, E., et al. 2017. An Ecoregion-Based Approach to Protecting Half the Terrestrial Realm. *BioScience* 67(6): 534–545. https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014. Data © RESOLVE, CC-BY 4.0.
