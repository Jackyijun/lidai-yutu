    前770 — 前476770–476 BC

    平王东迁 · 诸侯争霸King Ping moves east · the lords contend for hegemony

    以西周灭亡前一年（前772）开篇，从平王东迁、郑庄小霸，经齐桓、晋文、楚庄先后称霸，到吴越争雄、春秋之终，四十六个节点逐一展开列国疆域的消长。可在诸侯与共主两种着色之间切换：共主着色下，奉周王为共主的诸侯同用周色、加斜线，称王或不受周封者用本色。六十个诸侯国之外，赤狄、白狄、山戎、淮夷等七支部族以淡色虚线画出；都城、战役、会盟、关隘与山川都标出古名和今地。末节点与战国卷首节点的疆域、配色完全一致。Opening in 772 BC, the year before Western Zhou fell, and running from King Ping's move east and Duke Zhuang of Zheng's early ascendancy, through the successive hegemonies of Duke Huan of Qi, Duke Wen of Jin and King Zhuang of Chu, to the struggle between Wu and Yue and the end of the period, 46 snapshots trace the rise and fall of each state's territory. The map can be colored by state or by suzerain: in suzerain coloring, the states that acknowledge the Zhou king as suzerain share the Zhou color with hatching, while those that claim kingship or were never enfeoffed by Zhou keep their own. Alongside the 60 states, seven tribal peoples (among them the Red Di, the White Di, the Mountain Rong and the Huai Yi) are drawn in pale dashed outline; capitals, battles, covenants, passes, mountains and rivers carry their historical names and modern locations. The last snapshot has exactly the same borders and colors as the first snapshot of the Warring States volume.

凡例Notes

  1. 各卷疆域据正史与谭其骧《中国历史地图集》相应各册大意推定，仅为示意。Territories in each volume are inferred in outline from the standard histories and the corresponding volumes of Tan Qixiang's The Historical Atlas of China (Zhongguo lishi ditu ji); they are approximate.
  2. 地名皆作「古名 → 今地」对照，对照表所列经纬度可在任何地图应用中定位。Every place is given as historical name → modern location; the coordinates listed in each gazetteer can be looked up in any map app.
  3. 卷次按时代先后排列。每卷各自成页、各自更新，可单独分享；本页只作目录。Volumes are ordered by era. Each is a page of its own, updated separately and shareable on its own; this page serves only as the table of contents.
  4. 时间长卷上的朝代分段为通行断限，相邻朝代交替之年取整处理。The dynasty bands on the timeline follow conventional period boundaries, with the transition years between dynasties rounded.
